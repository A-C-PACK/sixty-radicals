PICK A VOICE

Five preset voices reading the same six samples: one character for each tone, a radical's name and a full sentence. On the site, the single characters and the names are what learners would hear.

“Unchecked” marks a take that did not pass the automatic check (speech recognition for the syllables, a pitch contour for the tone). The tone check could not track the three men's lower voices reliably, so a miss there may be the check's fault and your ear is the better judge. Eric is different: the recogniser misheard his words in most takes.
